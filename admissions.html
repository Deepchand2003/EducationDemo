How to Apply

Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to join Excellence Academy

1

Online Application

Complete our online application form with your personal details, academic history, and program preferences.

15-20 minutes
2

Document Submission

Upload required documents including transcripts, identification, and recommendation letters.

1-2 days
3

Application Review

Our admissions committee reviews your application and documents thoroughly.

2-3 weeks
4

Interview (if required)

Selected candidates may be invited for an interview, either in-person or virtual.

30-45 minutes
5

Admission Decision

Receive your admission decision via email with next steps for enrollment.

1 week
6

Enrollment & Fee Payment

Confirm your seat by completing enrollment and paying the required fees.

Before deadline
📋

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ High school diploma or equivalent (for undergraduate)
  • ✓ Bachelor's degree (for graduate programs)
  • ✓ Minimum GPA of 3.0 or equivalent
  • ✓ English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS for international students)
  • ✓ Standardized test scores (SAT/GRE/GMAT as applicable)
  • ✓ Age requirements vary by program
📄

Required Documents

  • ✓ Completed application form
  • ✓ Official academic transcripts
  • ✓ Government-issued ID or passport
  • ✓ Two letters of recommendation
  • ✓ Statement of purpose / personal essay
  • ✓ Passport-size photographs
  • ✓ Test scores (if applicable)
